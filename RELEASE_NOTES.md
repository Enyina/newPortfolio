# What's new on the portfolio

## October 30, 2026 — My AI engineering work is easier to see

**What changed**

The homepage now places my independent AI engineering system front and center. Visitors can explore a visual, four-scenario demonstration of how I investigate engineering problems, question AI recommendations, protect permission boundaries, and plan for recovery. The experience includes a real comparison from my personal Codex experiments and explains why a higher score was not enough to approve a change.

My recent work is now introduced with clearer, more confident language. The full case study, mobile layouts, project cards and share previews give visitors a stronger understanding of how I approach difficult engineering challenges.

**Why it matters**

A recruiter, collaborator or client can see how I think, what I built, and what I can prove—without needing access to private code. The demonstration makes complex technical work understandable without overselling experiments.

**What to know**

The interactive examples do not connect to a live AI assistant. The private source remains private, the improvement study is inconclusive, and some approvals and recovery checks still need to be demonstrated. New automated checks are included, but publication on the live site must be independently verified.


## October 17, 2026 — A face behind the work

**What changed**

The opening section now includes my existing professional photograph, positioned beside my introduction on larger screens and beneath it on smaller screens.

**Why it matters**

People visiting the portfolio can recognize the engineer they might work with, without losing the dark-and-orange design.

**What to know**

## October 17, 2026 — A clearer picture of my work

**What changed**

The portfolio now highlights five recent pieces of work: pharmacy ordering, safer payments, voice-assisted ordering, a clothing-design prototype, and a personal approach to improving work with AI.

The older applications have moved into an optional “Earlier projects” area. Their original pages and source links remain available.

The work history now reflects the latest résumé. Visitors can also open a clean, printable résumé page and save it as a PDF from their browser, without a personal phone number appearing on the page.

The contact form has also returned alongside the direct email link, so visitors can choose how to start a conversation.

**Why it matters**

Someone considering a job offer or project can see the problem, the contribution and the limitations of each piece of work much more clearly.

**What to know**

These changes have passed automatic checks, but a live production check on Netlify is still outstanding. The clothing designer is labeled a prototype, the AI workflow is still in progress, and some career measurements still need independent confirmation. The private editing dashboard is not available yet.
